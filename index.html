About Me من أنا

I am a Data Analyst and Business Information Systems (BIS) undergraduate graduating in June 2027. I use SQL, Power BI, Excel, and Python to clean, model, analyze, and present data into clear, actionable business answers.

My practical projects cover the full data lifecycle: architecting relational data warehouses in Microsoft SQL Server with automated Python ETL pipelines, building interactive Power BI dashboards based on star-schema models with advanced DAX, and developing predictive ML models for risk assessment and price forecasting. I have also completed intensive professional training through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) as a Data Analyst Specialist and the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI) in AI for Business.

I am actively seeking Data Analyst or Business Intelligence internships and junior roles based in Alexandria, Egypt, or remote roles open to candidates based in Egypt.
