About Me من أنا

Hi, I'm Abdelrahman — a Data Analyst and Business Intelligence Specialist dedicated to bridging the gap between raw, complex data and high-impact business decisions. With a strong foundation in Business Information Systems, I help organizations eliminate data ambiguity by delivering end-to-end analytical solutions that uncover growth opportunities, optimize operations, and drive profitability.

My workflow covers the entire data lifecycle. Using SQL, Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn), and Advanced Excel (Power Query, Power Pivot), I extract, clean, and structure high-volume datasets into reliable data models. From there, I leverage Power BI and advanced DAX to design intuitive, interactive dashboards that allow stakeholders to monitor core KPIs in real time and significantly reduce manual reporting overhead.

Beyond descriptive reporting, I apply statistical analysis and machine learning techniques to move businesses from reactive tracking to proactive forecasting. By combining technical precision with commercial storytelling, I ensure that every algorithm, data model, and visualization directly answers a critical business question.

Let's connect if you are looking for a results-driven analyst who can translate your numbers into a clear competitive advantage.

Heart Disease End-to-End Prediction & Analysis تحليل وتوقع مخاطر أمراض القلب (نظام متكامل)

Developed a comprehensive End-to-End Machine Learning & Business Intelligence project for predicting and analyzing heart disease risk using the CDC BRFSS dataset containing 319,795 patients.

Key Features & Technical Highlights:

  • Data Warehouse Architecture: Built a complete Medallion Architecture (Staging, Bronze, Silver, Gold) in SQL Server.
  • Automated ETL & Advanced SQL: Robust Python ETL pipeline with logging, along with 20+ complex SQL queries (CTEs, Window Functions) for analytics.
  • Power BI Dashboard: Interactive dashboard for patient segmentation (K-Means), risk factor analysis, and healthcare insights.
  • ML Pipeline & Explainable AI: Evaluated 6 ML models, optimized via Optuna (LightGBM V2: ROC-AUC = 0.84), integrated with SHAP for model interpretability.
  • Streamlit Predictor: Interactive web app for real-time patient risk assessment with dynamic SHAP analysis.

تطوير مشروع متكامل للتعلم الآلي وذكاء الأعمال لتحليل وتوقع مخاطر أمراض القلب، بناءً على مجموعة بيانات CDC BRFSS التي تشمل 319,795 مريضاً.

أبرز الميزات والتقنيات المستخدمة:

  • بنية مستودع البيانات: بناء بنية Medallion متكاملة (Staging, Bronze, Silver, Gold) باستخدام SQL Server.
  • خطوط ETL و SQL المتقدمة: خط معالجة (ETL) آلي باستخدام بايثون مع نظام تسجيل، بالإضافة إلى أكثر من 20 استعلام SQL معقد للتحليلات.
  • لوحة بيانات Power BI: لوحة تفاعلية لتقسيم المرضى (K-Means) وتحليل عوامل الخطر والرؤى الصحية.
  • التعلم الآلي وتفسير الذكاء الاصطناعي: تقييم 6 نماذج وضبطها باستخدام Optuna، مع دمج SHAP لتفسير القرارات.
  • تطبيق ويب Streamlit: تطبيق تفاعلي لتقييم المخاطر اللحظية للمرضى مع تحليل SHAP الديناميكي.
